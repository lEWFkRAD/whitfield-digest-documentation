# Whitfield County 2024 Tax Digest — Documentation Stack

**Status:** Public documentation package (public record review).  
**Prepared:** 2026-09-04, updated 2026-09-11.  
**Purpose:** Explain the 2024 digest breakdown to residents while preserving the evidence trail and the limits of what is known. All content is drawn from public records, audited statements, and reproducible arithmetic; human-clarification items are labeled as such.

- **Claims matrix:** `claims-matrix.md` — claim-by-claim status, support, limits, and upgrade path.
- **Calculations:** `calculations.md` — all material arithmetic with inputs and formulas.
- **Source register:** `source-register.md` — document IDs, locators, URLs, local exhibit names, and hashes.

- **Integrity manifest:** `MANIFEST.sha256` — SHA-256 hashes for durable package contents.

## Evidence labels

- **PRIMARY / AUDITED:** inspected government or audited record.
- **REPORTED:** named statement in contemporaneous reporting; underlying record may still be missing.
- **DERIVED:** arithmetic reproduced from cited inputs.
- **LEAD / ALLEGATION:** testable information not established by the public record.
- **OPEN:** missing record or unresolved question.

## The central clarification

The county finance director’s original rounded **“about $4 million”** point referred to **reduced fiscal capacity / anticipated use of reserves**, not a separate digest-loss figure. The official budget counterpart is the FY2026 resolution’s exact **$4,888,626 planned use of General Fund balance**. It is approved budget authority, not a realized loss and not proof of digest causation.
