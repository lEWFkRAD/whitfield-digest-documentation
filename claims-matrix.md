## Wording controls

- Say **“deficiency of revenues under expenditures,”** not “operating gap,” for the ACFR fund-flow line.
- Say **“planned use of fund balance,”** not “loss,” for $4,888,626.
- Say **“borrowed and repaid,”** not “lost,” for TAN principal.
- Say **“refund funding / budget authority,”** not “net loss,” for $1.956M unless payment records are obtained.
- Say **“public-utility ratio deficiency,”** not a failure of all assessment ratios.
- Attribute reported claims to the named speaker and publication.
